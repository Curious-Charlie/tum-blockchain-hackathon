Agents pay invoices on signed exchange rates, and get paid only on proof.

Lena runs a small coffee roastery in Munich. Her suppliers invoice in euros, she holds dollars, and she wants to pay when EUR/USD is right: without watching charts, and without giving an agent a wallet it could empty.

THREE RULES, ENFORCED BY CODE
01
Only the supplier in the order The payee comes from Lena's address book, never from the model. The contract can pay no one else.
02
Only on an API3-signed rate Right Airnode, right request, at most 5 minutes old, under her limit. Four forged responses were rejected on chain.
03
Only after proof The Keeper's fee waits in Masumi escrow until it posts a hash of the payment, which Lena's agent checks on Base Sepolia.
WHO IT'S FOR Small importers paying suppliers abroad in another currency, and the finance agents that run their invoices.
THE PROBLEM A bank limit order is slow and costly for a €400 invoice. An agent trusted blindly could fake the rate, pay the wrong account, or charge for work it never did.
HOW IT RUNS Lena's finance agent, a Sokosumi Coworker, hires one of three Keepers over x402 by price and on-chain record. The Keeper watches signed rates and executes on Base Sepolia.
BUSINESS MODEL Keepers charge per executed invoice (1–4 tUSDM in the demo), nothing for watching. A platform takes a share of Keeper fees or charges for the finance agent.
